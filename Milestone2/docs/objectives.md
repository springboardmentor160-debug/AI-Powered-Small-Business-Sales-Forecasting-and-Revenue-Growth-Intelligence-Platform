# MarketMind AI — Milestone 1 Objectives

## Sales Forecasting
Use historical sales, dates and revenue to support future sales planning.

## Customer Segmentation
Use purchase frequency, spending/value and customer activity to identify behavioral groups.

## Churn Prediction
Use inactivity, order frequency and engagement-related features to identify customers at risk.

## Product Recommendation
Use purchase history and product combinations to suggest relevant products for cross-sell and upsell opportunities.

## Anomaly Detection
Use transaction, revenue and inventory patterns to identify unusual business activity.

## Basic Workflow

Business uploads CSV
→ data is validated and cleaned
→ data is stored in the database
→ analytics services read the database
→ results are shown on role-specific dashboards
→ reports can be generated/exported in later milestones.
