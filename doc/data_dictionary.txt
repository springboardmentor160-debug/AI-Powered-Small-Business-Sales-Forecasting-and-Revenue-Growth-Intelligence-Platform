MARKETMIND AI - DATA DICTIONARY

1. CLEAN RETAIL SALES DATASET
File: clean_retail_sales.csv

InvoiceNo
- Unique invoice/transaction number.

StockCode
- Unique code identifying the product.

Description
- Name/description of the product.

Quantity
- Number of units purchased.

InvoiceDate
- Date and time of the transaction.

UnitPrice
- Price of one unit of the product.

CustomerID
- Unique identifier of the customer.

Country
- Country of the customer.

Revenue
- Total revenue generated by the transaction.
- Formula: Quantity × UnitPrice


2. CLEAN CUSTOMER DATASET
File: clean_customers.csv

CustomerID
- Unique customer identifier.

Country
- Customer's country.

Total_Orders
- Number of unique invoices/orders made by the customer.

Total_Quantity
- Total number of products purchased by the customer.

Total_Spending
- Total amount spent by the customer.


3. CLEAN PRODUCT DATASET
File: clean_products.csv

StockCode
- Unique product identifier.

Description
- Product name/description.

Total_Quantity_Sold
- Total number of units sold for the product.

Total_Revenue
- Total revenue generated by the product.
