MarketMind AI - Project Objectives

1. Analyze retail sales data to understand business performance.

2. Track sales, customers, products, and revenue.

3. Forecast future sales using machine learning.

4. Segment customers based on their purchasing behavior.

5. Identify customers who may stop purchasing.

6. Recommend relevant products based on customer purchasing patterns.

7. Detect unusual sales transactions and possible anomalies.

8. Present important business information through an interactive dashboard.

9. Provide useful insights that help small businesses make data-driven decisions.
