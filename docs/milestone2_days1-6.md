# MarketMind AI — Milestone 2 Documentation (Days 1–6)

## Overview
This document covers the implementation of **Milestone 2 (Days 1–6)** for MarketMind AI, introducing **Customer Segmentation** (K-Means & Hierarchical Clustering) and **Time-Series Sales Forecasting** (Prophet 30-Day Forecast).

---

## Day 1–2: Customer Segmentation Setup

### Customer Feature Extraction
Customer-level features were calculated directly from actual historical sales data without fabricating behavior:

1. **`purchase_frequency`**: Total number of orders placed by the customer.
2. **`purchase_value`**: Total monetary revenue generated by the customer ($quantity \times unit\_price$).
3. **`customer_activity_days`**: Active date span in calendar days ($\text{max\_date} - \text{min\_date} + 1$).

#### Data Table (`data/processed/customer_features.csv`):
| Customer ID | Purchase Frequency | Purchase Value ($) | Activity Days |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **C001** | 2 | 180.00 | 2 |
| **C002** | 2 | 144.00 | 3 |
| **C003** | 2 | 240.00 | 4 |
| **C004** | 1 | 85.00 | 1 |
| **C005** | 1 | 48.00 | 1 |

### Feature Scaling & K-Means Clustering
- **Scaler**: `sklearn.preprocessing.StandardScaler` applied to numerical feature matrix $X$.
- **Algorithm**: `sklearn.cluster.KMeans(n_clusters=4, random_state=42, n_init=10)`.
- Output column generated: `cluster`.

## Day 3–4: Hierarchical Clustering & Named Customer Segments

### Hierarchical Agglomerative Clustering
- **Algorithm**: `sklearn.cluster.AgglomerativeClustering(n_clusters=4, linkage='ward')` executed on the same scaled feature matrix.
- Output column generated: `cluster_hierarchical`.

### Dendrogram Visualization
- Generated using `scipy.cluster.hierarchy` with Ward linkage.
- Saved under: `reports/dendrogram.png`.

### Cluster Analysis & Business Segment Naming
Clusters were mapped to business-readable names based on observed average feature values rather than arbitrary cluster IDs:

| Segment Name | Customer Count | Avg Purchase Value | Avg Frequency | Avg Activity Days | Assigned Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Regular Customers** | 1 | $180.00 | 2.0 | 2 days | C001 |
| **Occasional Shoppers** | 1 | $144.00 | 2.0 | 3 days | C002 |

## Day 5–6: Sales Forecasting Setup (Prophet)

### Time-Series Preparation & Daily Revenue
Sales transactions were aggregated by order date to form daily revenue time-series data stored at `data/processed/daily_revenue.csv`:

| Date (`ds`) | Daily Revenue (`y`) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **2026-01-05** | $255.00 |
| **2026-01-06** | $45.00 |
| **2026-01-07** | $174.00 |
| **2026-01-08** | $85.00 |
| **2026-01-09** | $48.00 |
| **2026-01-10** | $90.00 |

### Missing Date Check
- Evaluated full calendar date range from `2026-01-05` through `2026-01-10`.
- **Recorded days**: 6.
- **Missing dates count**: 0.
- Daily continuity is intact across the available sequence.

### Prophet Model Setup & 30-Day Forecast
- Formatted input dataset into `ds` (datestamp) and `y` (target revenue value).
- Fitted `prophet.Prophet` model and predicted 30 future days (`periods=30`).
- Generated fields:
  - `ds`: Forecast date
  - `yhat`: Central point forecast
  - `yhat_lower`: Lower bound of uncertainty interval
  - `yhat_upper`: Upper bound of uncertainty interval

### Visualizations
- Forecast Chart saved to: `reports/forecast_chart.png`.
- Trend/Seasonality Components saved to: `reports/forecast_components.png`.

> - Data was processed as-is without fabricating transactions or artificial accuracy.
> - Forecast uncertainty bounds are wide due to the short historical baseline.
